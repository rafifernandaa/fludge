# Demo Script: System Refinement & Operator Workflow
*Estimated Time: 2.5 - 3 minutes*

## Scene 1: Anomaly Detection
**(Visual: The GIS Map focused on the river networks. Normal conditions shown.)**
**Operator (Voiceover):** "Our network relies on real-time river sensors to detect anomalies instantly. Here, sensors detect a sudden rise in water levels at the Katulampa gate."
**(Visual: A sensor icon flashes orange on the map. The dashboard updates to show the threshold breach.)**
**Operator:** "The system automatically compares the live water level against our pre-defined safety thresholds, highlighting the critical sectors in real-time."

## Scene 2: Verification
**(Visual: Operator clicks on the 'CCTV Live' toggle. Map populates with CCTV feeds. They click a flashing CCTV node.)**
**Operator:** "Before escalating, we verify the situation. By toggling our new *CCTV Live feed*, we can immediately access ground-truth visual data to confirm the rising water levels reported by the sensors."
**(Visual: A mock CCTV feed pop-up appears showing high water levels.)**

## Scene 3: Impact Analysis & Alerting
**(Visual: The map switches to the 'Pop Density' and 'Flood Depth' layers. A Gumbel distribution graph flashes on the sidebar.)**
**Operator:** "Once confirmed, the system calculates the exact affected area and the estimated time of flood arrival using extreme value theory. The operator can instantly issue a targeted alert to the affected neighborhoods."
**(Visual: Operator clicks 'Issue Alert' or highlights an RT unit. A confirmation notification appears.)**

## Scene 4: Evacuation Deployment
**(Visual: The operator clicks the 'GPS Assets' toggle. Asset icons (trucks, boats) appear on the map. The Evacuation panel is opened.)**
**Operator:** "With alerts issued, we deploy the evacuation team. We can now track our *GPS Logistics Assets*—such as rescue boats and trucks—live on the map. We assign a team to the nearest optimal haven."
**(Visual: Operator clicks 'Dispatch Team' on the Evacuation Route panel. A new entry is added to the Evacuation Log.)**

## Scene 5: Monitoring & Reporting
**(Visual: Operator opens the Evacuation Logs modal. They click 'Export PDF'.)**
**Operator:** "As the situation develops, we monitor the evacuation progress. A key new feature is our *Comprehensive Evacuation Log*, which records deployment times, routes, and personnel. When conditions are safe, we export this detailed report for stakeholders with a single click."
**(Visual: The PDF report preview is shown briefly. The map returns to a stable state.)**
**Operator:** "From detection to resolution, the system ensures a fast, verified, and well-documented response."
