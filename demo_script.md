## 🎙️ FULL NARRATION & ACTION SCRIPT

### 🟥 BEAT 1: THE PROBLEM (0:00 - 0:35)
* **Visual Action:** Start on the Fludge Command HUD landing page. Point to the live Jakarta WIB clock (UTC+7). Click the **Simulation Panel** on the right and select the **"Monsoon Flood"** preset.
* **Speaker Voiceover:**
  > "DKI Jakarta faces catastrophic flash flooding across more than 30,000 neighborhood sectors, known as RTs.
  > During monsoons, traditional single-threaded CPU GIS systems take over **22 seconds** to calculate spatial joins between river sensors, BMKG weather radar grids, and DEMNAS elevation models.
  > In emergency operations, a 22-second delay creates critical communication blackouts when lives are on the line. Meet **FLUDGE**—the GPU-accelerated flash flood command center."

---

### 🟩 BEAT 2: THE ACCELERATION (0:35 - 1:10)
* **Visual Action:** Click the **Data Pipeline** icon on the left sidebar (`/data_pipeline`) to display the 7-Step End-to-End Architecture diagram. Then click the **Python Core** icon (`/python_core`) to show the CUDA RAPIDS script.
* **Speaker Voiceover:**
  > "To eliminate this bottleneck, Fludge offloads the entire 7-step hydrological pipeline to **NVIDIA RAPIDS cuDF** running on Google Cloud GPUs.
  > 1. Ingesting 30,000 RT geometries and BMKG radar telemetry into BigQuery.
  > 2. Parallel Point-in-Polygon catchment matching via cuSpatial.
  > 3. Vectorized Inverse Distance Weighting rainfall interpolation.
  > 4. Gumbel Extreme Value Theory (EVT) exceedance modeling.
  > 5. Composite risk scoring.
  > 6. Dijkstra route graph building.
  > 7. Sub-second dispatch rendering.
  > Processing time plummets from 22,400 milliseconds to **under 4.5 milliseconds**—a **5,000x speedup**."
